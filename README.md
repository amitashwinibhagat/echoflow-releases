## ⌨️ Two shortcuts run everything

**Dictate — `⌥ Space`**

1. Hold `⌥ Space`. The overlay appears instantly.
2. Speak naturally. Echo Flow reads the room: Mail becomes email, Slack stays casual, code stays verbatim.
3. Release. Polished text lands exactly where your cursor is.

**Rewrite — `⌥⇧Space`**

1. Select any text in any app.
2. Press `⌥⇧Space`. Pick Fix Writing, Sound Pro, Make It Shorter, Bullet Points, or another palette action.
3. Echo Flow replaces the selection (or copies the result where the app blocks replacement).

**Clinical Note mode — `⌥⌘N`** (for therapists, clinicians, anyone writing structured notes)

Speak a 2–10 minute post-session summary and get back an organized SOAP, DAP, BIRP, GIRP, Progress, or Intake note, or your own template. Sections you skip are omitted. You review every note before it pastes. Recordings are deleted after transcription; only the raw transcript stays on your Mac for a retention period you choose.

Walkthrough with screenshots: [echoflow.one/how-to-use.html](https://echoflow.one/how-to-use.html)

## 🔒 Privacy you can verify, not just trust

- Dictated audio, transcripts, and rewritten text stay on your Mac. They are not uploaded for polishing.
- Transcription runs on-device (bundled speech engine on Apple Neural Engine, or Apple's built-in recognizer if you prefer).
- Echo Flow AI runs locally after a one-time setup download (~380 MB).
- No account. No cloud sync. No training on your content.
- Network calls are limited to: setup downloads, update checks, license validation, purchase redirects, and optional email signup.
- Clinical Note recordings are deleted as soon as they are transcribed.

## ❓ FAQ

<details>
<summary><strong>How does Echo Flow work?</strong></summary>

Hold <code>⌥ Space</code>, speak, release. Your words are transcribed on-device, polished with context-aware tone, and pasted where your cursor is. Select any text and press <code>⌥⇧Space</code> to rewrite it in place.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Is my voice data private?</strong></summary>

Yes. Dictated audio, transcripts, and rewritten text stay on your Mac and are not uploaded for polishing. Servers are contacted only for setup downloads, update checks, licensing, purchase redirects, and optional email signup.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Does it work offline?</strong></summary>

After setup, dictation and rewriting run locally. The one-time Echo Flow AI setup download, update checks, license validation, purchase redirects, and optional email signup need internet.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Which apps does it work with?</strong></summary>

Anywhere you can type: Gmail, Slack, Notion, VS Code, Messages, Word, Apple Mail, Notes, AI chats. Rewrite works on selected text in any app.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What Mac do I need?</strong></summary>

macOS 15 or later on Apple Silicon (M1 or newer). Echo Flow AI needs a one-time setup download, then runs locally.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Is there a subscription?</strong></summary>

No. 14-day dictation trial, then a one-time purchase for lifetime dictation access with free 1.x updates. The rewrite palette stays available after trial expiry. Earlybird $69.99, then $139.99.
</details>
